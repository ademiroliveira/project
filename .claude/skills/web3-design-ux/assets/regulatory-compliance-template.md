## Compliance Best Practices

1. **Always visible**: Place disclaimers where users will see them
2. **Before action**: Show risks before user commits
3. **Cannot skip**: Require acknowledgment for high-risk actions
4. **Plain language**: Legal but understandable
5. **Versioned**: Date disclaimers for updates
6. **Logged**: Record user acceptance
7. **Jurisdiction-aware**: Show relevant disclaimers per region
8. **Mobile-friendly**: Readable on all devices
9. **Translated**: Provide in user's language
10. **Accessible**: Screen-reader compatible
